Baraa Portfolio

An Interactive Personal Portfolio with an AI-Powered Assistant

Live Demo: https://baraas-folio-spark.lovable.app

1. Problem

Recruiters and potential freelance clients need a simple way to understand a professional's skills, experience, and projects. A traditional CV may not provide enough context about practical work or allow visitors to explore projects interactively.

This portfolio solves that problem by presenting professional information, skills, and projects in one organized, interactive website.

2. Target Users

- Recruiters and hiring managers.
- Potential freelance clients.
- Companies looking for QA, AI, or automation-related skills.
- Professional collaborators interested in exploring projects.

3. Goal

The goal is to build a professional online presence that showcases Baraa Mahmoud Abu Watfa's experience and projects, improves information accessibility, and makes communication with potential clients and employers easier.

4. Requirements

Functional Requirements

- Display personal and professional information.
- Present skills and work experience.
- Organize projects into dedicated sections.
- Allow visitors to explore individual project details.
- Provide interactive navigation and animations.
- Include an AI-powered chatbot.
- Allow visitors to contact Baraa through email.
- Support desktop and mobile screens.

Non-Functional Requirements

- User-friendly interface.
- Responsive and accessible design.
- Consistent visual identity.
- Reliable chatbot behavior.
- Secure handling of AI credentials.
- Clear and maintainable project documentation.

5. User Flow

1. The visitor opens the portfolio.
2. The visitor learns about Baraa and her professional background.
3. The visitor explores skills and work experience.
4. The visitor opens projects to learn about their purpose and implementation.
5. The visitor asks the AI assistant questions about the portfolio.
6. The chatbot provides relevant information and supports the visitor's next steps.
7. The visitor contacts Baraa through the available email option or visits her LinkedIn profile.

6. Data

The portfolio uses the following information:

- Personal and professional information: Name, email, LinkedIn profile, experience, and skills.
- Project information: Project names, descriptions, objectives, workflows, tools, and links.
- Chatbot input: Questions submitted by website visitors.
- Chatbot output: AI-generated answers based on the information available to the assistant.
- Contact information: Details used to help visitors communicate with Baraa.

Only relevant information should be provided to the AI assistant. Private information and confidential project data must be protected.

7. AI Feature

AI-Powered Portfolio Assistant

The portfolio includes an AI-powered chatbot that helps visitors discover professional information through conversational questions.

Features:

- Answer questions about professional skills and experience.
- Explain portfolio projects and their purposes.
- Help visitors find relevant information.
- Guide visitors toward available contact options.
- Support a more interactive website experience.

Why AI was selected:

AI allows visitors to ask questions naturally instead of searching manually through every section. This makes information discovery more convenient and gives the portfolio an interactive component.

Testing and Validation:

The chatbot was tested successfully. During testing, it processed the interaction and sent an email successfully.

The successful test confirmed that the tested chatbot-to-email workflow was functioning as expected.

10. Short Reflection

1. What problem was the portfolio designed to solve?

The portfolio was designed to bring my professional information, skills, and projects together in one organized platform. It helps recruiters and potential clients understand my capabilities more easily than a traditional CV alone.

2. Who is the primary user?

The primary users are recruiters and potential freelance clients who want to explore my experience, review my projects, and evaluate whether my skills match their needs.

3. What was the most important decision made during the design process?

One of the most important decisions was organizing the website into clear sections and providing dedicated project details. This makes the information easier to navigate and helps visitors understand the purpose and value of each project.

4. Where was AI used, and why was this point selected?

AI was used in the portfolio chatbot to help visitors explore my skills, experience, and projects through natural-language questions. I selected this feature because it improves information discovery and makes the portfolio more interactive. I also tested the chatbot and confirmed that the email workflow worked successfully.

5. What changed between the first version and the final version?

The portfolio developed into a more structured and interactive website, with organized content, project presentations, animations, and an AI-powered chatbot connected to an email workflow. Testing helped confirm that the chatbot interaction and email delivery worked as expected.

6. What did you learn about building applications using AI?

I learned that AI-assisted development can speed up building applications and help turn ideas into working solutions. However, clear requirements, thoughtful design, testing, and human verification remain essential. A feature should not be considered complete simply because it appears in the interface; it must also work correctly when tested.

Conclusion

Baraa Portfolio demonstrates how professional presentation, interactive project documentation, and an AI-powered assistant can work together to create a useful digital experience.

The project combines AI-assisted development with practical testing and validation, showing the importance of usability, reliability, and continuous improvement.
